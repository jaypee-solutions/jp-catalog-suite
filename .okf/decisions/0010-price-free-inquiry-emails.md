# Overview

The New inquiry and Inquiry confirmation emails trigger on the pending-to-Inquiry transition. Dedicated HTML and plain templates show products, SKU, quantity, item details, the customer message and customer details without prices. WooCommerce settings control recipients and enabled state; uninstall removes both settings.
