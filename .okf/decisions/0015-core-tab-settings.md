# Overview

The settings options `jps_cs_core_tab_{description,additional_information,reviews}_{title,priority,hidden}` control WooCommerce's built-in Core tabs shop-wide. Settings are applied after Product tabs are added and preserve WooCommerce defaults when the title and priority are empty. Product tabs cannot replace Core tabs.
